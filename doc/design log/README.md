# Design log

One entry per design decision, numbered in the order it was taken. Rejected designs get an
entry as well, since the reason something was dropped is usually the useful part.

| # | Entry | Joint | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [0001](0001-j1-belt-driven-base.md) | Belt driven J1 base, prototype 1 | J1 | Rejected |

## Writing an entry

Follow the shape of entry 0001:

1. What was built or proposed, with the numbers taken from CAD in a table.
2. Why the decision went the way it did, one short section per reason, each with its own
   arithmetic.
3. What changes as a result.
4. What is worth keeping from the design that was dropped.
5. What is still open.

Say which numbers are measured and which are assumed. Most of the numbers here are still
assumed, and that is worth being upfront about.
