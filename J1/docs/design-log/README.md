# Design log

One entry per significant design decision, numbered in the order it was taken. Rejected
designs get an entry too — the reasoning is the point.

Each entry states what was built or proposed, the numbers behind the decision, what was
decided, and what is carried forward.

## Writing a new entry

Copy the structure of entry 0001:

1. **Summary** — the decision and the two or three reasons for it, in a paragraph.
2. **What was built** — measured values from CAD, in a table. No prose numbers.
3. **Why** — one collapsible section per reason, each with its own arithmetic.
4. **Decision** — what changes.
5. **What to carry forward** — the parts of the rejected design that were right.
6. **Open questions** — the assumptions the decision still rests on.

State assumptions explicitly and separate what was measured from what was estimated.
